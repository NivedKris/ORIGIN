## 6. Publications & Research

### Accepted Paper at ISCLS 2026
*   **Paper Title:** "A computational study of how Sanskrit-specific morphophonological phenomena behave under noisy spoken input"
*   **Authors:** Nived Krishna, John Martin, Manu Madhavan
*   **Paper Link:** [View Published Paper](https://lnkd.in/dH4XX-8f) (Icon: `fa-file-text-o`)
*   **Abstract/Description:** This work investigates how Sanskrit phenomena such as *sandhi* and *samāsa* interact with ASR noise, and why aggregate metrics like Word Error Rate (WER) often fail to predict downstream usability in speech-driven access systems. Using the *Charaka Saṃhitā* as a testbed, we construct a controlled oracle–ASR benchmark and show that boundary-sensitive errors disproportionately degrade retrieval performance even when overall transcription accuracy appears similar.
